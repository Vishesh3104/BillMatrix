Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about BillMatrix — setup, features, GST compliance, and more.

How do I get started with BillMatrix?
+
Sign up for a free trial — no credit card needed. Add your business details, GST number, and start creating invoices within minutes.
Is BillMatrix fully GST compliant?
+
Yes. BillMatrix supports CGST, SGST, IGST, CESS calculations. You can generate e-invoices, e-Way bills, and directly file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B returns.
Can I use BillMatrix on mobile?
+
Absolutely. BillMatrix works on web, Android, and iOS with full feature parity and real-time sync across all devices.
Can I migrate from Tally or Busy?
+
Yes. We provide guided migration support to move your products, customers, and historical data from Tally, Busy, or any other billing software.
Is my data secure?
+
Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit, stored on Indian servers, and backed up daily. We are SOC2 compliant and follow all DPDP Act guidelines.
